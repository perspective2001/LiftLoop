Achievements

Core achievements show goals and progress. Special is a discovery type—not a tier above Mythic.
How LiftLoop achievements work
Level + RankEvery finished workout adds one level. Starting Line, Builder, Grinder, Veteran, Iron, Elite, and Legend are your long-term rank—not achievement rarity.
CoreVisible goals with progress. These are the achievements counted toward Trophy Case completion.
Rarity tierBronze → Silver → Gold → Mythic. Mythic is always the highest achievement tier.
SpecialA hidden training pattern LiftLoop discovers. Specials can be Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Mythic; “Special” is a category modifier, not a fifth rarity tier.
MomentsWhen a repeatable Special happens again, LiftLoop records the real event in that badge’s history instead of creating disposable duplicate badges.
Gilded + AscendedRepeatable Specials can visually evolve after enough distinct Moments. The app does not show a next-unlock counter, so it rewards natural training instead of farming.
Century prestigeCentury Club unlocks at 100 workouts. At 200, 300, 400 and every later hundred, the same Mythic badge advances and records another Century Mark.
Working Weight PRYour first full-prescription result sets the baseline. A later sustainable improvement across every prescribed set at target reps is a genuine Working Weight PR.
