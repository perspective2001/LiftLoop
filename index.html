Pull Day

Back + Biceps + Core

0%
FORM KEYS
  • Start by pulling your shoulders down away from your ears.
  • Drive your elbows down and avoid swinging to create momentum.
SETS / ROUNDS
FORM KEYS
  • Keep your upper arms mostly still beside your torso.
  • Curl without swinging your hips, then lower the bar under control.
SETS / ROUNDS
FORM KEYS
  • Keep your chest tall and spine neutral.
  • Pull the handle toward your torso, then let the arms extend without rounding hard forward.
SETS / ROUNDS
FORM KEYS
  • Alternate sides without twisting or leaning to help the weight up.
  • Keep the elbow close to your side and control the lowering phase.
SETS / ROUNDS
FORM KEYS
  • Keep your chest up with only a slight lean back.
  • Pull the bar toward the upper chest by driving your elbows down.
SETS / ROUNDS
FORM KEYS
  • Keep a neutral hammer grip and curl toward the opposite shoulder.
  • Avoid rotating your torso to move the weight.
SETS / ROUNDS
FORM KEYS
  • Brace before each rep and move through a comfortable range.
  • Lower slowly so gravity does not do the whole eccentric.
